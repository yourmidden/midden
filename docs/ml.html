The models midden runs

midden works out what each thing you save is about with small models that run on your device. They ship inside the extension and it can't fetch others, so what you save is never sent to a model somewhere else.

Job Model Licence
Text embeddings bge-small-en-v1.5 MIT
Finding text PP-OCRv4 detection Apache-2.0
Reading text PP-OCRv5 English recognition Apache-2.0
Speech to text whisper-tiny.en Apache-2.0
Speech detection Silero VAD v5 MIT
Images by content SigLIP base patch16-224 Apache-2.0
QR codes zxing-cpp, or the browser's BarcodeDetector Apache-2.0
  • English only, for now: the embedder, text reading and speech models are all English.
  • The phone runs none of them. It searches what your other devices have already worked out.
