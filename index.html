Yours, not ours

midden is our kick at something simple: bringing you value without you selling what you put in. Your thoughts should be your own. So midden isn't a service. It does its thinking on your device, and what you do in it is only there to help you.

  • On your devices. What you save, and everything midden works out from it.
  • Sync through your own Drive. Encrypted on your device first.
  • We don't harvest it, and we don't sell it. No analytics on what you save, no training on it, no ads.
  • It keeps working without us. If we disappeared tomorrow, midden and your heap would still be there. Export always works.

Filmed in midden itself, on a made-up heap.

A midden is a heap nobody sorted: the pile archaeologists dig through, years later, to learn how people lived.

Most of what we save is like that. A link to read later, a screenshot of a receipt, a note at midnight. Filing it all is a chore nobody keeps up. So don't. Keep it, and let midden do the digging.

Throw it on the pile

Nothing to file, nothing to name. Wherever you are, it's one move, then back to what you were doing.

In midden

  • Type a thought and press Enter. Add a #tag if you feel like it.
  • Paste or drop a file or picture anywhere on the page.
  • Talk. Record a voice note and midden writes out what you said.
  • Doodle it, when words won't do.

On any web page

  • Right-click to send the page, a link or the text you've selected.
  • Snag an image.
  • Snip part of the screen, error message and all.

On your phone

  • Share from any app on Android.
  • Select text in an app and send it across.
  • Take a photo of the whiteboard.
  • Say it on a walk.

It keeps the context. midden holds on to as much as it can about each thing: when you saved it, how, and the page it came from. Then it reads what's inside: the words in a screenshot, what you said in a voice note, the link in a QR code.

On a project? Set a tag once and everything you add gets it, from anywhere, until you clear it.
